JumpStart's Mission

Jumpstart is a Non-profit organization that has been using the arts, drama and guidance to heal and break barriers since 2002. Jumpstart has serviced over 13,978 youth throughout the tri-state area. Our mission overall, is to redirect the minds of the youth in America today and to create the character, self-esteem and self-awareness necessary to initiate positive change amongst the at risk urban youth.
